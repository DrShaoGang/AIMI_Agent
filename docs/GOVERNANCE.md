# Governance Principles

## Purpose

AIMI is intended to support professional metadata work, not replace professional judgment. Governance requirements are therefore embedded throughout diagnosis, enrichment, validation, and release.

## Core safeguards

### Human oversight

The system should route uncertain, sensitive, or high-impact recommendations to qualified reviewers. Human review criteria should be explicit and consistently applied.

### Bounded authority

Agents should operate only within documented permissions. Field-level controls should distinguish between analysis, recommendation, and authorized modification.

### Provenance

Each proposed or applied change should identify its source records, authoritative references, responsible component, and relevant processing context.

### Confidence reporting

Agent outputs should include structured confidence information that is meaningful to reviewers and downstream systems. Confidence should not be presented as a substitute for validation.

### Decision traceability

The system should preserve enough information for librarians to understand how a recommendation was produced, what evidence informed it, and which validation steps were completed.

### Standards alignment

Enhancements should be constrained by applicable schemas and grounded in established vocabularies, ontologies, authority files, and identifier systems.

### Auditability

Agent actions, transformations, review decisions, and exceptions should be logged in a form suitable for operational auditing and evaluation.

## Evaluation questions

- Are readiness scores and diagnostic findings reliable and consistent?
- Can reviewers understand and verify recommendations?
- Are enhancements structurally and semantically valid?
- Are confidence signals appropriately calibrated?
- Do permissions prevent unauthorized or uncontrolled modifications?
- Can the workflow be transferred to another institutional context?
- Do users trust the system for the tasks it is designed to support?

## Living document

This document records initial governance commitments from the project proposal. It will be refined through implementation, pilot testing, external review, and community feedback.
